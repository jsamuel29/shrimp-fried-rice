The 20-Minute Shrimp Fried Rice

A fast, affordable meal solution for college students to save money and eat well with limited resources.

The Secret to the Recipe

Pro-Tip:Use day-old rice! This is the secret to the dish because it ensures the grains stay firm and don't get mushy in the wok.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb. large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 3 cups of day-old cooked white rice
  • 1/2 cup frozen peas and carrots, thawed
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced, and 2 green onions, sliced
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce and 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 2 large eggs, lightly beaten, and 2 tbsp vegetable oil for frying

The Wok Flow

  1. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a large Wok or skillet over high heat until shimmering.
  2. Pour in the beaten eggs and scramble quickly; remove from the pan and set aside while still slightly soft.
  3. Add the remaining oil to the pan and sear the shrimp 1-2 minutes per side until pink and opaque; remove and set aside.
  4. Add the day-old rice to the pan, breaking up any clumps with a spatula until the grains are coated and starting to toast.
  5. Stir in the peas, carrots, garlic, and cooked shrimp.
  6. Pour the soy sauce and sesame oil over the mixture, tossing constantly for 2 minutes to mimic the fast-paced nature of stir-fry cooking.
  7. Fold in the scrambled eggs and sliced green onions, then serve immediately.
