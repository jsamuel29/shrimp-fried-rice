The 20-Minute Shrimp Fried Rice

A fast, affordable meal solution for college students to save money and eat well with limited resources.

The Secret to the Recipe

Pro-Tip:Use day-old rice! This is the secret to the dish because it ensures the grains stay firm and don't get mushy in the wok.

Ingredients

  • Cooked shrimp (peeled and deveined)
  • Day-old white rice
  • Frozen peas and carrots
  • Eggs (beaten)
  • Soy sauce and sesame oil
  • Green onions

The Wok Flow

  1. Heat the wok or large skillet over high heat with a tablespoon of oil.
  2. Scramble the eggs quickly and set them aside.
  3. Saute the shrimp until pink, then remove.
  4. Add the day-old rice and vegetables, breaking up any clumps.
  5. Stir in the soy sauce, sesame oil, shrimp, and eggs.
  6. Toss everything together until heated through and serve immediately.
